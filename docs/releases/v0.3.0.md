IMMCA v0.3.0 lets people follow Canadian immigration changes without coming back to the site, installs as an app, and adds an open-source, privacy-preserving way to count usage.

**Live site:** https://ellensyuu.github.io/IMMCA/

## Subscribe in your calendar

- One-click subscriptions for **Apple Calendar** (Mac, iPhone), **Google Calendar**, **Outlook.com**, **Outlook for work or school**, and **Windows** (paste the link into Outlook or the Calendar app).
- Two calendars to choose from: **only when something changes** (one event per new Express Entry round, program deadline with a reminder three days before, the CRS rule change, or an official page change), or **every morning** (a short digest at 8:00 Eastern).
- In English, French and Chinese, rebuilt every day by the daily workflow. Also available as RSS/Atom feeds.
- Feeds are checked in tests with an independent iCalendar parser (ical.js).

## Install as an app

- IMMCA is now an installable web app (PWA) on iPhone, Android, Mac and Windows: full screen, offline, refreshed daily.
- An install button where the browser supports it, and short instructions for iPhone and Android otherwise.

## Usage counts, our own and open source

- A small counter (`counter/`, Cloudflare Worker + D1) records page views, calendar subscriptions, app installs and calendar-feed readers.
- No cookies, no stored IP addresses, nothing from the form; unique visitors are counted with a hash salted daily and deleted the next day. See PRIVACY.md.
- Daily totals are copied into `stats/` and shown as README badges. The counter needs a free Cloudflare account to go live (see `counter/README.md`); until then everything else works and the badges say "not set up yet".

## Also

- The daily monitor ignores maintenance and outage banners, which caused the first false alarm.
- Express Entry round names are cleaned of stray punctuation from IRCC's feed.
