IMMCA v0.2.0 makes results easier to act on and keeps the site current every day without anyone pushing a change.

**Live site:** https://ellensyuu.github.io/IMMCA/

## Clearer results

- **Answer first.** A "Where to focus" summary names up to three routes worth pursuing, the one requirement blocking the nearest route, and the dates that matter to you.
- **Four dimensions instead of one label.** Each route now shows separately whether you meet the rules, whether it is open (or its 2026 spaces are limited or used up), how you compare with the latest official cutoff where one exists, and how solid the evidence is (verified, approximated, unconfirmed, or official page changed).
- **Scenario lab.** See what one change at a time — NCLC 7 in French, another year of Canadian work, a job offer, a nomination — does to your CRS and which routes it opens.
- **A calmer page.** Profile steps collapse into one-line summaries, the score card shows the score and your place in the pool with details on demand, and route rows are reduced to the transit line and three short pills.

## Updated automatically every day

- Express Entry rounds and the CRS pool distribution refresh daily from IRCC's feed.
- Every official page cited by a rule is checked daily. A change flags the affected programs on the site and opens a GitHub issue with the exact text diff; rules are never rewritten automatically.
- The site redeploys every morning, so the "last checked" date is always current.
