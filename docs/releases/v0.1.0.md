First public release of IMMCA — a browser-only screener that shows which routes to Canadian permanent residence are open to you, what is still missing, and the official page behind every rule. English, Français and 中文.

**Live site:** https://ellensyuu.github.io/IMMCA/

## What's in it

- **56 programs** across federal Express Entry (CEC, FSW, FST and every 2026 category draw), the Atlantic, rural and francophone pilots, Québec's PSTQ and the 2026 PEQ reopening, and the nominee streams of every province and territory that runs one.
- **Rules checked against official sources on 2026-10-27**, with each program linking to the pages it was verified against.
- **CRS calculator under both rule sets** — today's grid and the Labour Market Integration factor that starts on **2026-11-03** — plus the single changes that would raise your score most.
- **Your score in the real pool**, using IRCC's published distribution of 228,724 candidates, refreshed weekly from IRCC's own feed.
- **Indicative Arrima score** for Québec's PSTQ.
- **Test-score conversion** for IELTS, CELPIP, PTE Core, TEF Canada (both scales) and TCF Canada, including the Québec French scale.
- **Time-aware rules:** the PEQ window closes itself after 2026-10-31, and the PEQ's 2025-11-19 snapshot rule is evaluated against today's date.

## Quality

- Every program spec was audited line by line against the research log by a reviewer that did not write it; each finding is pinned by a regression test.
- 53 tests, including a check that every requirement and hint renders completely in all three languages.
- No server, analytics or accounts: nothing anyone types leaves their browser.

## Known limitations

- Saskatchewan Tech Talent's 32 occupations are approximated by NOC broad category 2.
- Prince Edward Island's detailed Labour Impact thresholds could not be read on the official site.
- Ontario has not yet published invitation rounds for its new Workforce Priority stream.
- Québec's PEQ notice and stream page disagree on whether workers need a Québec diploma.
- Business and investor streams are out of scope.

See [`docs/research/`](https://github.com/EllenSyuu/IMMCA/tree/main/docs/research) for the full evidence log. IMMCA is not legal advice; always confirm on the official page before acting.
