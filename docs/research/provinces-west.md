# Western & Northern PNP streams — students/graduates & workers

Verified: 2026-10-27. Method: WebSearch + WebFetch of official pages (welcomebc.ca, alberta.ca, saskatchewan.ca, immigratemanitoba.com, yukon.ca, immigratenwt.ca). Where an official page could not be fetched, the secondary source is named and the item is flagged `verify: secondary`.

Conventions for the screener:
- `enhanced` = Express Entry-linked (nomination adds 600 CRS). `base` = paper/portal, non-EE.
- `job_offer.teer` = allowed NOC 2021 TEER levels.
- `clb` = minimum in each of four skills unless stated.
- "Graduation in province" = whether a credential from an in-province institution is *required* (not merely scored).
- Criteria are minimums for eligibility; selection is competitive (EOI/draws), so meeting criteria does not mean an invitation.

## 1. British Columbia (BC PNP)

General 2026 context (welcomebc.ca news, 2026-04-23 "Priority and program changes"; Skills Immigration Program Guide effective 2026-06-10):
- All dedicated **student/graduate streams are gone**: International Graduate (last invitations Nov 2024), International Post-Graduate (applications accepted until 2025-01-07). Announced replacement Bachelor's/Master's/Doctorate streams were **not launched**; on 2026-04-23 BC said it **will not launch new student streams**. BC/Canadian study now only earns registration points.
- **Entry Level and Semi-Skilled (ELSS) officially closed** (last ITAs 2024-12-10). The BC PNP Tech draws ended 2024-12-03 (tech occupations remain eligible under Skilled Worker but no longer get dedicated draws).
- Selection now through three priorities: **Care** (36 occupations: health, childcare, education, veterinary), **Build** (9 construction trades), **Innovate** (High Economic Impact: wage-threshold or top-points draws, all sectors).
- Ineligible occupations/employers list expanded; >=35% of nominations targeted outside Metro Vancouver.
- Application fee $1,750 (from 2026-01-22). Registration pool: 7,695 registrations at 2026-10-06.

### BC-SW — Skills Immigration: Skilled Worker
- Official name: Skills Immigration – Skilled Worker (with optional Express Entry BC option)
- Type: base, or enhanced if registered under EEBC (needs FSW/FST/CEC eligibility + EE profile + job seeker code).
- Status: **open for registration**; invitations only through targeted draws (Care/Build/Innovate). No general draws.
- Eligibility:
  - job_offer: required; TEER 0–3; full-time (avg >= 30 h/wk year-round); indeterminate (some Appendix A occupations may accept limited term); employer in BC >= 1 yr, >= 5 FT employees (Metro Vancouver) or >= 3 (elsewhere); no staffing agencies; not on ineligible-occupation list (e.g., several admin, sales/service supervisor, real estate, religious occupations).
  - work_experience: >= 24 months full-time (or equivalent) skilled (TEER 0–3) experience in last 10 years, anywhere; paid; **work on a study permit does not count** (paid full-time skilled co-op after program completion may).
  - clb: TEER 2–3 -> CLB 4 all skills; TEER 0–1 -> no test required unless claiming points.
  - education: no stream minimum beyond job requirements. **BC graduation not required.**
  - wage/income: wage within WorkBC/Job Bank range for the NOC; family income must meet minimum for family size and area (Metro Van vs rest of BC) — table not extracted.
  - age: none.
  - residence: intent to live/work in BC.
- Registration score (max 200): work experience max 40 (5+ yrs = 20; +10 if >=1 yr in Canada; +10 if currently FT with supporting employer); education max 40 (PhD 27, Master's 22, Bachelor's/PG cert 15, associate/diploma 5; **+8 post-secondary completed in BC**, +6 in Canada outside BC, +5 BC professional designation); language max 40 (CLB 9+ = 30 ... CLB 4 = 5; +10 bilingual); wage max 55; area max 25 (wage and area tables not extracted).
- Selection: registration -> score -> targeted invitations.

## 2. Alberta (AAIP)

General 2026 context (alberta.ca/aaip-updates, aaip-processing-information updated 2026-10-21):
- Single **Worker EOI** system (since 2024-09-30) for all worker streams; $135 Worker EOI fee from 2026-04-07; EOIs valid 1 year; editable since 2026-05-26; wage/hours fields required since 2026-02-25.
- Worker EOI points grid (max 100, doc dated 2026-09-28): education level max 12 (PhD 12, Master's 10, Bachelor's/trade 7, diploma 4); **education location: Alberta 10, other province 6**; language max 10 (CLB 6+ = 10, 5 = 8, 4 = 5) + 3 bilingual; total experience max 11 (12+ mo = 11); Canadian experience: 6+ mo in Alberta 10, other province 6; age max 5 (21–34 = 5); family in Alberta 8; permanent FT Alberta offer 10; Rural Renewal endorsement / tourism association / police offer 6; job outside Calgary & Edmonton CMAs 5; regulated occupation with licence 10. **Minimum CLB 4 in all skills to submit an EOI.**
- 2025: Family-connection/occupation-in-demand EE pathway ended (2025-03-17); Alberta did not join the 2025 federal work-permit public policy. 2025-11-18: Rural Renewal changes effective 2026-01-01 (community endorsement caps, 1-yr validity of endorsement letters, TEER-based endorsement model, valid work permit required).
- Draws are irregular and consider factors beyond score; AAIP does not disclose all draw parameters.
- No dedicated graduate stream for workers; Alberta graduates get reduced requirements under AOS and Rural Renewal, plus EOI points. (Graduate Entrepreneur is business.)

### AB-AOS — Alberta Opportunity Stream
- Type: base.
- Status: **open via Worker EOI; invitation-only**. 2026 allocation 3,562; 3,273 issued; 289 remaining (2026-10-21). Near exhaustion.
- Eligibility:
  - Residence/permit: living and working in Alberta; valid permit at submission AND assessment (maintained/implied or restoration status NOT accepted). Eligible permits: positive-LMIA, listed LMIA-exempt, **PGWP from an Alberta publicly funded institution**, certain IRCC open permits. TFWs working in another province ineligible.
  - job_offer: required; full-time (>= 30 h/wk) from Alberta employer in the **same occupation as current job**; TEER 0–5 except ineligible list (e.g., K-12 teachers, some ECEs, teacher assistants, real estate agents, home child care/support workers, many TEER 5 service roles); not part-time/casual/seasonal, contractors, agency, home-based, remote-from-outside-AB. Compulsory trades need AIT certificate; childcare needs ECE Level 2/3.
  - work_experience: standard — 12 months FT in Alberta within last 18 months, OR 24 months in Canada/abroad within last 30 months, in current occupation. **Alberta-PGWP holders: 6 months FT in Alberta within last 18 months.** Study co-op excluded (except paid FT Alberta co-op for PGWP holders).
  - clb: TEER 0–3 = 5; TEER 4–5 = 4; NOC 33102 = 7.
  - education: high school + ECA (unless Canadian credential). For PGWP route: credential from Alberta publicly funded institution, Advanced Education-approved (post-dip/post-bacc cert >= 1 yr, diploma >= 2 yrs, bachelor's, graduate); occupation must relate to Alberta field of study (post-bacc certs started on/after 2019-04-01: must relate to prior foreign field).
  - age/funds: none stated.
- 2026 draws (min score / #): 2026-10-02 54/123; 2026-09-23 58/113; 2026-09-01 56/575 (more back to 2026-01-26 on the page).
- URL: https://www.alberta.ca/aaip-alberta-opportunity-stream-eligibility
- Uncertainty: no published minimum wage requirement on page; wage now collected in EOI.

### AB-RRS — Rural Renewal Stream

## 3. Saskatchewan (SINP)

General 2026 context (saskatchewan.ca pages; CIC News 2025-12 summary of SINP announcement):
- Allocation announced 4,761 (40.5% below 2024's 8,000); processing-stats page now shows 5,261.
- **Priority sectors** (>= 50%): healthcare, agriculture, skilled trades, mining, manufacturing, energy, technology — no intake windows, no "6 months left on permit" rule, can apply from overseas.
- **750 spaces reserved for Saskatchewan DLI graduates** with job offers in a priority sector (based on employer's sector, not field of study).
- **Capped sectors** (max 25%): Accommodation & Food Services (NAICS 72) 15%, Trucking (48–49) 5%, Retail (44–45) 5%. EPAs only during intake windows (Jan, Mar, May, Jul, Sep, Nov) and only for current employees with <= 6 months left on their work permit and valid status (no restoration/maintained-without-permit).
- **PGWP holders who studied outside Saskatchewan can no longer use Saskatchewan Experience**; PGWP holders may only use Student, Health Talent, Agriculture Talent, Tech Talent, and ISW-Employment Offer. Spousal OWP holders lost access to several pathways.
- Job-offer categories require employer **Employer Position Assessment (EPA)** via OASIS (replaced older "JAO" terminology).
- Processing (Q3 2026): ~2 weeks for most categories.

### SK-STU — Saskatchewan Experience: Students
- Type: base.
- Status: **open, first-come (EPA-gated)**; capped-sector job offers subject to intake windows.
- Eligibility:
  - education: completed certificate/diploma/degree from a **Saskatchewan DLI (required)**; no minimum program length stated; no time-since-graduation window stated; PGWP based on non-Sask education -> ineligible.
  - permit: valid PGWP, study permit (only if job offer in priority sector), or CUAET OWP.
  - work_experience: >= 6 months (780 h) paid work **in Saskatchewan** related to field of study (part-time/non-consecutive OK; on-campus, off-campus, co-op, fellowships, PGWP work count; remote-from-outside-Sask excluded).
  - job_offer: permanent, full-time, TEER 0–3 or designated trade, related to field of study, requiring post-secondary; EPA approved before applying. Food & beverage servers -> use Existing Work Permit.
  - clb: none unless TEER 4–5 designated trade (then CLB 4).
  - residence: must reside in Saskatchewan.
- URL: https://www.saskatchewan.ca/residents/moving-to-saskatchewan/live-in-saskatchewan/by-immigrating/saskatchewan-immigrant-nominee-program/browse-sinp-programs/applicants-with-saskatchewan-experience/students
- Uncertainty: the page has no effective date; the "Assess your eligibility" page says health care workers are now also eligible here.

### SK-EWP — Saskatchewan Experience: Skilled Worker with Existing Work Permit
- Type: base.
- Status: open (EPA-gated; capped sectors via intake windows — 2026 windows filled within hours/days; Sep 14 Retail window had 30 left at last check).
- Eligibility: >= 6 months (780 h) consecutive FT (30+ h/wk) paid work with the supporting Sask employer on an eligible work permit; **PGWP, spousal OWP and study-permit holders NOT eligible** (and PGWP/SOWP experience not counted); TEER 0–3 or designated trade (TEER 0–5 for CUAET, LMIA-based, Francophone Mobility permits; truck drivers 73300 and F&B servers 65200 only on those permits); permanent FT offer with EPA; CLB 4 for TEER 4–5 (page ambiguous whether CLB 4 applies generally); health occupations (NOC broad category 3) excluded -> Health Talent.
- URL: .../applicants-with-saskatchewan-experience/applicants-with-existing-work-permit

### SK-ISW-EO — International Skilled Worker: Employment Offer
- Type: base.
- Status: open (EPA-gated).
- Eligibility: permanent FT TEER 0–3 Sask job with EPA; >= 12 months (1,560 h) paid experience in intended occupation in last 10 years; **PGWP/SOWP holders need >= 6 months with the offering employer** (counts toward 12); CLB 4; >= 60 points on SINP grid (education, experience, language, age, Sask connections); licensing if regulated. Truck drivers and health occupations (category 3) excluded. Lost-status applicants in capped sectors ineligible. Graduation in Sask not required.
- URL: .../applicants-international-skilled-workers/international-skilled-worker-with-employment-offer

## 4. Manitoba (MPNP)

General 2026 context:
- **Career Employment Pathway (IES) closed "effective immediately" — MPNP notice 2026-06-11** (official URL found: https://immigratemanitoba.com/2026/06/important-update-for-manitoba-provincial-nominee-program-international-education-stream-ies-career-employment-pathway-cep-candidates/ — page fetch failed; details from CIC News 2026-06 report, `verify: secondary`). Note: the CEP eligibility page on immigratemanitoba.com still showed criteria without a closure banner when fetched.
- Manitoba graduates are now channelled into **Skilled Worker in Manitoba (SWM)**, with **dedicated draws prioritising Manitoba post-secondary graduates**.
- 2026 allocation 6,239. Monthly draws through Skilled Worker Stream; Express Entry linkage is optional (EE profile + job seeker code declared in EOI).
- Contact by webform only since 2026-03-02.
- Manitoba EOI scores out of 1,000 (grid not fetched).

### MB-SWM — Skilled Worker Stream: Skilled Worker in Manitoba
- Type: base, or enhanced if candidate declares valid EE profile + job seeker code.
- Status: **open via EOI; regular draws** (21 draws in 2026 to Oct 22).
- Eligibility (official eligibility page):
  - Ongoing Manitoba employment: valid work permit + full-time long-term offer from the Manitoba employer **after >= 6 months continuous FT employment with that employer** (**>= 12 months if graduated from a post-secondary program in another Canadian province/territory**).
  - Excludes self-employment, unauthorized work, and work during full-time study (incl. co-op).
  - Employer must be incorporated/registered, operate in Manitoba with an established place of business.
  - Must hold all qualifications/licences for the position.
  - Language: "sufficient" for job and economic establishment — **no fixed CLB on page** (CEP had CLB 7).
  - Settlement Plan; Manitoba ties stronger than any other province.
  - No settlement-funds or study requirement (per secondary comparison).
  - Ineligible: refugee claimants, live-in caregivers in Canada, TFWs working/living outside Manitoba, spouses of Canadian citizens/PRs, refused by MPNP in last 6 months, active application in another program, self-employed/owners/contractors, work-permit-exempt workers.
  - TEER: no TEER restriction stated on page (unverified; secondary sources say In-Demand Occupations List applies for some draws).
- 2026 draws (official pages for #266 and #269; #279–#282 via immigration.ca reports, `verify: secondary`):
