Overview
The New Brunswick Strategic Initiative is for French-speaking people who want to live and work in New Brunswick. They must have the skills, education and work experience needed to help the province’s economy grow.
\n"}}" id="text-2a9a55e9c2" class="cmp-text">
The New Brunswick Strategic Initiative is for French-speaking people who want to live and work in New Brunswick. They must have the skills, education and work experience needed to help the province’s economy grow.
Eligibility
To be eligible, you must:
\n \n be at least 19 years old
\n have at least a Canadian high school diploma or its foreign equivalent
\n have at least a level 5 in all four French language skills according to the Niveaux de compétence linguistique canadiens (NCLC)
\n have at least one year of qualifying work experience from within the past five years
\n - not required if you graduated from a New Brunswick post-secondary institution
\n meet the proof of funds requirement
\n - not required if you are living in New Brunswick or have a valid job offer from a New Brunswick employer
\n score at least 65/100 points based on the selection factor grid
\n \n In addition, there are specific requirements for each pathway of the New Brunswick Strategic Initiative.
\n Francophone Workers in New Brunswick
\n To be eligible under this pathway, you must:
\n \n be working in, or have accepted, a permanent, full-time, and non-seasonal (year-round) position for an eligible employer who has been actively operating in New Brunswick for the past 12 months, providing goods or services
\n meet your New Brunswick job requirements, according to the National Occupational Classification and other applicable laws and regulations
\n have been living in New Brunswick for at least six months
\n - not required if you were offered a job through a recruitment mission led by the Government of New Brunswick
\n \n Read the Francophone Workers in New Brunswick application guide .
\n New Brunswick Francophone Priorities
\n To be eligible under this pathway, you must meet the definition of one of the two priorities:
\n 1. Francophone graduate
\n You must:
\n \n be living in New Brunswick
\n have completed an in-person program of study of at least 1-year long at either the Université de Moncton or the Collège communautaire du Nouveau-Brunswick (CCNB)
\n have been physically present in the province for the entire duration of your program
\n \n 2. Letter of interest
\n You must have received a letter of interest from Immigration New Brunswick
\n Read the New Brunswick Francophone Priorities application guide .
\n Francophones Working Remotely in New Brunswick
\n To be eligible under this pathway, you must:
\n \n have been living in New Brunswick for 12 months
\n have been working remotely for a Canadian employer located outside of Quebec during this entire period
\n \n Read the Francophones Working Remotely in New Brunswick application guide .
