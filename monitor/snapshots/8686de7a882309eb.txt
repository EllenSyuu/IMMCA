Overview
This stream is for people who work in New Brunswick or have a full-time, non-seasonal job offer from an eligible employer. These workers have skills that employers in the province need.
\n"}}" id="text-4cffad4abd" class="cmp-text">
This stream is for people who work in New Brunswick or have a full-time, non-seasonal job offer from an eligible employer. These workers have skills that employers in the province need.
Eligibility
To be eligible, you must:
\n \n be at least 19 years old
\n have a level 4 in all four language skills according to the Canadian Language Benchmarks (CLB)
\n have the support of an eligible employer who has been actively operating in New Brunswick for the past 24 months, providing goods or services
\n meet your New Brunswick job requirements, according to the National Occupational Classification and other applicable laws and regulations
\n \n In addition, there are specific requirements for each pathway of the New Brunswick Skilled Worker stream.
\n New Brunswick Experience
\n To be eligible under this pathway, you must also:
\n \n be working full time in a non-seasonal position for the employer who is supporting your application
\n have at least six months of full-time work experience with the same employer
\n have lived in New Brunswick for the past six months
\n have at least a high school diploma
\n \n Read the New Brunswick Experience application guide (PDF 335 KB) .
\n New Brunswick Graduates
\n To be eligible under this pathway, you must also:
\n \n have completed a program of study that is eligible for a post-graduation work permit at a designated learning institution located in New Brunswick
\n be working in, or have accepted, a full time non-seasonal position for the employer who is supporting your application
\n have lived in New Brunswick for the past six months
\n \n Read the New Brunswick Graduates application guide (PDF 337 KB) .
\n New Brunswick Priority Occupations
\n To be eligible under this pathway, you must also:
\n \n have accepted a full-time, non-seasonal job offer from the employer who is supporting your application
\n have at least one year of paid work experience related to this position
\n have at least a high school diploma
\n \n Additionally:
\n \n the offer must be a direct result of a recruitment mission led by the Government of New Brunswick
\n the National Occupational Classification for the position must be one of the priority codes specified in the guide
\n \n Read the New Brunswick Priority Occupations application guide (PDF 335 KB) .
