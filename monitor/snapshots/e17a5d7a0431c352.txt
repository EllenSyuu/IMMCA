Rural Community Immigration Pilots: Who can apply - Canada.ca
Who can apply
As of January 1, 2027, the Rural Community Immigration Pilot will close.
If your community recommendation is dated December 31, 2026, or earlier, you must apply for permanent residence before it expires. If you don’t have a recommendation under the pilot, explore the new permanent Rural Community Immigration Program .
We’ll continue processing applications we’ve already received. If you’re eligible, you can still apply for a work permit so you can work while we process your application.
To apply for permanent residence under this pilot, you must
obtain a valid recommendation certificate from a participating community
have a valid job offer from a designated employer in the community
have at least 1 year (1,560 hours) of related work experience in the past 3 years (opens in a new tab)
prove your language abilities by taking an approved test (opens in a new tab)
have a Canadian educational credential or the foreign equivalent (opens in a new tab)
prove you have enough money (opens in a new tab) (opens in a new tab) to support yourself and your family
