The acceptance of employer designation applications under the Atlantic Immigration Program will remain on pause for the remainder of 2026.

Candidate endorsement application – Selection process
\n"}}" id="text-799217b9c5" class="cmp-text">
Candidate endorsement application – Selection process
Meeting the eligibility requirements of the program does not guarantee that an endorsement application will be selected for processing. Due to limited endorsement allocations and continued high demand, not all eligible applications can be selected.
\r\n As demand continues to exceed available allocations, Immigration New Brunswick prioritizes candidates who demonstrate a strong likelihood of long-term economic establishment and retention in the province.
\r\n Primary selection factors include:
\r\n \r\n duration of employment in New Brunswick
\r\n demonstration of economic establishment and attachment to the province
\r\n employment or a job offer in a priority sector, namely healthcare, education, construction
\r\n \r\n Individuals with expiring work permits are encouraged to explore options to maintain their legal authorization to work in Canada. Employers and workers should consider available federal pathways, including the Temporary Foreign Worker Program (TFWP) and Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA) to support work permit extensions, where applicable.
