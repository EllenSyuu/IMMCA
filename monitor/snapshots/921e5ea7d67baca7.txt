Overview
This stream is designed for highly skilled foreign workers. If you have a valid profile in the federal Express Entry pool and meet New Brunswick’s criteria, you can apply for a nomination from Immigration New Brunswick. This will give your Comprehensive Ranking System score a boost, leading to an invitation to apply for permanent residence from Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada.

This stream is designed for highly skilled foreign workers. If you have a valid profile in the federal Express Entry pool and meet New Brunswick’s criteria, you can apply for a nomination from Immigration New Brunswick. This will give your Comprehensive Ranking System score a boost, leading to an invitation to apply for permanent residence from Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada.
Eligibility
To be eligible, you must:
\r\n \r\n be between 19 to 55 years old
\r\n have a valid profile in the federal Express Entry pool
\r\n have at least a Canadian high school diploma or its foreign equivalent
\r\n have at least a level 7 in all four language skills according to the Canadian Language Benchmarks (CLB)
\r\n have at least one year of continuous work experience from within the past 10 years in one NOC code in a TEER category 0, 1, 2 or 3
\r\n score at least 67/100 points based on the selection factor grid
\r\n \r\n In addition, there are specific requirements for each pathway of the New Brunswick Express Entry stream.
\r\n Employment in New Brunswick
\r\n To be eligible under this pathway, you must also:
\r\n \r\n be working full time in a non-seasonal position: \r\n in a TEER category 0, 1, 2, or 3
\r\n for an eligible employer who has been actively operating in New Brunswick for the past 24 months, providing goods or services
\r\n \r\n
\r\n meet your New Brunswick job requirements, according to the National Occupational Classification and other applicable laws and regulations
\r\n have been living and working full time in New Brunswick for the past 12 months:
\r\n - in a TEER category 0, 1, 2, or 3 only
\r\n - with one or many New Brunswick employers
\r\n - while authorized to work in Canada
\r\n \r\n Read the Employment in New Brunswick application guide (PDF 374 KB) .
\r\n New Brunswick Interest
\r\n To be eligible under this pathway, you must also:
\r\n \r\n have received a letter of interest in your federal Express Entry profile
\r\n meet the federal proof of funds requirement
\r\n \r\n Read the New Brunswick Interests application guide (PDF 333 KB) .
